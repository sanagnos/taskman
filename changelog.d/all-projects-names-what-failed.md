- `taskman status` names what it failed to fetch because of a bug, and exits 1: with `--all-projects`, a project or ticket (`failed_projects`, and `failed` under a project, in `--json`), which it left out; for a stacked ticket, its base's PR (`failed` in `--json`), whose stack it showed as unknown.
